---

# 1. Company Definition

## Company
**Triply**

## What Triply is
Triply is a travel dating and pre-trip connection product built around **future destination + overlapping travel dates**.

## What Triply is not
Triply is not:
- a generic dating app
- a travel booking platform
- an itinerary planner
- a social feed
- a hostel/community app
- a portfolio project
- a side hustle

Triply is being built as a serious founder-led company.

---

# 2. Core Insight

Most dating products organize discovery around:

> **Where are you now?**

Triply organizes discovery around:

> **Where are you going, and when will you be there?**

That future-location layer is the product wedge.

---

# 3. Core User Problem

A traveler has an upcoming trip and may want:
- romance
- dating
- companionship
- someone to explore with
- a meaningful connection before arrival

Current alternatives are fragmented:
1. wait until arrival and use existing dating apps
2. manually change location in dating apps
3. join travel groups
4. meet people in hostels/events
5. use social travel communities
6. rely on chance

Triply is designed around the question:

> **Who compatible with me will be in the same destination during the same dates?**

---

# 4. Mission, Vision, Purpose

## Purpose
**Help people turn future travel into meaningful human connection.**

## Mission
**Help travelers meet compatible people before their trip begins.**

## Vision

# 5. Strategic Positioning

## Positioning statement
For independent travelers who are open to dating or meaningful connection during an upcoming trip, Triply helps them discover compatible people who will be in the same destination during overlapping travel dates.

Unlike mainstream dating apps organized around current location, Triply starts with the future trip itself.

## Strategic territory
**Connection before arrival**

## Core differentiation
**Destination + Dates + Compatibility + Before Arrival**

## Working tagline
**Meet before you go.**

# 6. Primary Audience

## Initial ICP
Independent leisure travelers, approximately 21–35 initially, who:
- travel solo or semi-solo
- are comfortable with dating/social apps
- plan upcoming trips with known dates
- are open to romance or meaningful connection
- use social media and travel content
- are comfortable creating a profile and future-trip record

## High-potential segments
1. Solo leisure travelers
2. Digital nomads
3. Backpackers / hostel travelers
4. City-break travelers
5. Event/festival travelers

## Not initial focus
- families
- business-only travel
- local-only dating
- itinerary planning
- broad friendship-only communities
- users with no future trip intent

---

# 7. Product Thesis

The central product object is the **Trip**.

A Trip contains:
- destination
- arrival date
- departure date
- user
- visibility/safety controls
- status

The core loop is:

> **Account → Profile → Future Trip → Relevant Discovery → Like → Mutual Match → Chat**

The MVP succeeds if users repeatedly move through that loop.

# 9. Matching Logic

Initial matching eligibility should require:
- same destination
- overlapping travel dates
- compatible gender/orientation preferences
- age-range compatibility
- acceptable account status
- minimum profile completeness
- no block/report conflict

Advanced compatibility scoring comes later.

# 10. Marketplace Strategy

## Biggest strategic risk
**Liquidity fragmentation**

Effective supply is constrained by:

> active users × destination overlap × date overlap × preference compatibility × trust/safety eligibility

This means total user count is not the right marketplace-health metric.

## Core rule
**Density before scale**

## Launch model
Triply should launch by:
- destination
- travel window
- cohort

rather than globally.

Example:
> **Going to Bali this December? See who else is going when you are.**

# 16. Trust & Safety

Safety is a product system, not footer copy.

- reporting

- unmatching

- safe trip visibility

- no real-time precise location by default

- safety guidance

## Future systems

- optional identity verification
- verified traveler badge
- anti-scam detection
- risk scoring
- emergency/share-safety features

**Never trade safety for growth.**

# 24. Retention Model

Travel is episodic.

Therefore retention should include:
- return during active trip planning
- repeat visit to chats/matches
- creation of a second future trip
- reactivation for a new destination
- recurring travel behavior

Do not rely only on standard DAU/MAU thinking.

---

# 36. Founder Standard

Triply should only move forward through explicit reasoning.

For every major decision, ask:

1. What problem are we solving?
2. What evidence supports this?
3. What assumption are we making?
4. What metric will tell us if it is working?
5. What happens if the assumption is wrong?
6. Is this required for the core loop?
7. Does this improve liquidity, safety, connection, or retention?

If the answer is no, it probably does not belong in the current phase.

# 37. Final Strategic Summary

Triply has earned the right to move from validation into product building.

The company thesis is:

> **Future travel intent can become a dating and connection surface.**

The product thesis is:

> **A traveler creates a trip, sees compatible people whose destination and dates overlap, matches, and starts a conversation before arrival.**

The marketplace strategy is:

> **Concentrate supply before expanding geography.**

The brand strategy is:

> **Connection before arrival.**

The growth strategy is:

> **Build useful destination/date cohorts, not vanity traffic.**

The operating principle is:

> **Narrow the market → concentrate supply → create real connections → measure behavior → repeat → scale.**
